# Contact & Connect

> Reach out, ask questions, report broken links, or connect with the StaticStack maintainer and community through our Facebook Groups and GitHub repository.

---

## Why No Contact Form?

In keeping with the **StaticStack** philosophy—zero backend maintenance, zero spam, and open collaboration—we don't use a private email inbox or traditional contact form. Instead, all questions, feedback, and discussions happen openly through our **Facebook Groups** and **GitHub Repository** so the entire static web community can participate and benefit.

---

## 1. Connect on Facebook

Our official Facebook group is the fastest and friendliest place to message us, share your static website projects, ask integration questions, and connect with fellow web creators:

- **<a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/staticshark" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StaticStack Official Facebook Group</a>** — Official announcements, static web tool discussions, showcases, and direct community support.

---

## 2. Connect on GitHub

For technical feedback, bug reports, broken link fixes, or code contributions, GitHub is our primary point of contact:

- **<a href="https://github.com/E6WeB/htmlstatic" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub Repository (`E6WeB/htmlstatic`)</a>** — Browse the source code, star the project, or open a pull request.
- **<a href="https://github.com/E6WeB/htmlstatic/discussions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub Discussions</a>** — Ask architectural questions, suggest new categories, or start a community conversation.
- **<a href="https://github.com/E6WeB/htmlstatic/issues" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub Issues</a>** — Report a broken link, outdated pricing badge, or site bug.

---

## 3. Submit a Tool or Join the Forum

- **Want to suggest a static web tool?** Use our **[Submit a Tool](/submit)** page or review our **[Contributing Guide](/contribute)**.
- **Want to chat right here on the site?** Visit our **[Community Forum](/community)** to browse showcases and discussions.
