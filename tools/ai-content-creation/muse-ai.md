## Overview

Muse.ai is an ad-free video hosting and streaming platform built for websites that want clean, fast-loading video embeds without third-party ads or suggested-video distractions.

Alongside adaptive bitrate playback and custom player branding, Muse.ai indexes spoken words, on-screen text, and visual moments so visitors can search inside your videos and jump directly to the exact timestamp.

---

## Key Highlights & Features

1. **Ad-Free Responsive Embeds**: Drop-in `<iframe>` and script embeds that scale cleanly across desktop and mobile without recommended-video clutter.
2. **AI-Powered Video Search**: Search speech, text, objects, and scenes across single videos or entire embedded collections.
3. **Privacy & Domain Controls**: Restrict playback to specific domains, protect videos with passwords, and avoid third-party ad trackers.
4. **Interactive Collections & CTAs**: Embed searchable video playlists, chapters, captions, and clickable calls-to-action directly in the player.

## How It Works with Static Websites

Embed a Muse.ai video or collection directly into any static HTML page:

```html
<!-- Responsive Muse.ai video embed -->
<div style="position:relative; padding-bottom:56.25%; height:0; overflow:hidden; border-radius:12px;">
  <iframe
    src="https://muse.ai/embed/YOUR_VIDEO_ID"
    style="position:absolute; top:0; left:0; width:100%; height:100%; border:0;"
    allowfullscreen
    loading="lazy"
    title="Product Demo Video">
  </iframe>
</div>
```

## When to Use It

1. Landing pages, documentation sites, and portfolios that need ad-free 4K video hosting without managing raw `.mp4` or HLS buckets.
2. Course portals or video libraries on static sites where visitors benefit from searching inside video content.
