## Overview

Carrd is a lightweight web-based builder for creating clean, responsive one-page websites for profiles, landing pages, portfolios, and forms without writing code.

---

## Key Highlights & Features

1. **One-Page Focus**: Streamlined editor tailored for fast, focused single-page websites.
2. **Responsive Templates**: Dozens of starter templates that adapt automatically to desktop and mobile screens.
3. **Custom Domains & Embeds**: Pro plans support custom domains, forms, analytics, and custom HTML/JS embeds.
4. **Free Tier**: Build and publish up to three sites on `.carrd.co` subdomains at zero cost.
