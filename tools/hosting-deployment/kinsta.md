## How It Works with Static Websites

1. Log into your MyKinsta dashboard and navigate to **Static Sites**.
2. Click **Add site** and connect your GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repository.
3. Configure your build settings:
   - For vanilla static sites: set the publish directory to `./` or `public` with no build command.
   - For static site generators: enter your build command (e.g. `npm run build`) and output directory (e.g. `dist`).
4. Kinsta builds and deploys your site to Cloudflare's global CDN in seconds.
5. Add your custom domain under the **Domains** tab and point your DNS records.
