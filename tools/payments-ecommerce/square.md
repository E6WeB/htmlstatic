## Overview

Square provides no-code **Payment Links** and hosted checkout pages that let static website owners sell products, accept donations, collect invoices, or book paid appointments without building a backend. You can link directly to a Square checkout page from any HTML button or generate a QR code from your Square Dashboard.

Square supports credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App Pay, and Afterpay (Buy Now, Pay Later), and syncs online sales with in-person Square POS inventory if you also sell offline.

---

## Key Highlights & Features

1. **No-Code Payment Links & Buttons**: Create hosted checkout links, buy buttons, or donation links directly in the Square Dashboard.
2. **Modern Wallets & BNPL**: Accepts Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App Pay, Afterpay, and major cards out of the box.
3. **Unified Online & In-Person Inventory**: Syncs catalog items and orders with Square POS if you sell both online and in person.
4. **Zero Monthly Fee Option**: Start selling immediately on the Free plan and only pay per transaction.

---

## How It Works with Static Websites

Create a Payment Link in your Square Dashboard and attach it to any button or link in your static HTML:

```html
<a href="https://square.link/u/YOUR_PAYMENT_LINK_ID"
   target="_blank"
   rel="noopener noreferrer"
   class="square-buy-button">
  Pay with Square
</a>
```

---

## When to Use It

1. Selling physical goods, digital items, services, or accepting donations from a static site with zero backend code.
2. Businesses already using Square POS that want unified online and in-person inventory.
3. Static landing pages that need Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App Pay, and Afterpay support via a simple link.

## Pricing & Transaction Fees

- **Free Plan**: $0/month — **3.3% + $0.30** per online Payment Link / Checkout transaction
- **Square Plus**: $49/month per location — lowers online checkout rate to **2.9% + $0.30** per transaction
- **Square Premium**: $149/month per location — **2.9% + $0.30** online checkout rate with lower in-person rates and 24/7 support
- **Afterpay (BNPL)**: 6% + $0.30 per transaction
- **Payouts**: Standard next-business-day bank transfers are free (instant transfers carry a 1.95% fee)
