## Overview

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is a full-featured marketing and email delivery platform. It combines email marketing, transactional emails, SMS messaging, and signup forms under one roof.

Brevo offers customizable, embeddable subscription forms and an intuitive email designer, making it easy to capture leads on static landing pages.

---

## Key Highlights & Features

- **Generous Free Plan**: Send up to 300 emails per day for free.
- **Embeddable Forms**: Create styled signup widgets to embed on static pages.
- **Marketing Automation**: Trigger automated welcome sequences when users subscribe.
- **Transactional API**: Powerful SMTP and REST API for site notifications.

---

## How It Works with Static Websites

Embed a Brevo subscription widget onto your static site:

```html
<iframe width="540" height="305"
        src="https://my.brevo.com/forms/YOUR_FORM_ID"
        frameborder="0"
        scrolling="auto"
        allowfullscreen
        style="display: block;margin-left: auto;margin-right: auto;max-width: 100%;">
</iframe>
```

---

## When to Use It

- Growing businesses needing email marketing campaigns and automated sequences.
- Landing pages requiring lead capture forms with automatic contact list tagging.
- Projects needing both transactional email delivery and marketing newsletters.
