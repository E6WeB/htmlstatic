Submit a Tool

StaticStack is 100% community-curated and maintained on GitHub. Submissions are processed through GitHub Issues to ensure transparency, security, and spam prevention.

Propose a Tool or Service

Fill out the details below. Clicking "Continue to GitHub" will securely pre-fill an issue template on our official repository.

Must be an official HTTPS link with zero affiliate or referral codes.

Inclusion Guidelines

  • Relevant to Static Frontends: Must enhance HTML, CSS, and JS sites without requiring a traditional backend server.
  • Zero Affiliate Links: Direct official links only. Referrals or promotional campaign parameters are rejected.
  • Working & Maintained: Tools must be actively developed and reliable. Discontinued or broken tools will not be accepted.
  • Quality Over Quantity: We aim to provide the best tools in each category rather than an unfiltered directory.

Prefer opening a Pull Request?

You can edit README.md directly and submit a PR with your proposed addition.
